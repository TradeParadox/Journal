GXT strategy

One process. Four stages.

01

Read the market

Start with context. Know the trend, the key levels and whether the day is worth trading at all.

02

Wait for the GXT signal

No signal, no trade. The setup either meets the rules or it does not, and patience is part of the edge.

03

Define the risk

Entry, stop and target are set before the order goes in, with the size worked out from what you can afford to lose.

04

Journal and review

Every trade is logged and scored, so the weak habits show up in the numbers and not in your memory.
