I teach the part charts don't show.

Sreejith Trade Paradox started from one observation: the traders who last aren't the ones with the best entries. They're the ones with a plan for being wrong.

That plan is what I teach. How to size a position, where the stop goes, what to write in a journal, and how to review a bad week without blaming yourself or the market. Sessions cover Indian stocks and indices, crypto and forex, using real charts and the journal on this site.
